Literature and Tools

Form and Harmony

Phrase, sentence, period and cadence terms; Roman numerals.

  • Edward Aldwell, Carl Schachter and Allen Cadwallader, Harmony and Voice Leading, 5th ed. (Boston: Cengage, 2019).
  • L. Poundie Burstein and Joseph N. Straus, Concise Introduction to Tonal Harmony, 2nd ed. (New York: Norton, 2020).
  • Brellochs, “Dr. B Music Theory” lesson series (YouTube), from Chiyami’s handwritten notes: the order of topics and the definitions the pages follow.

Galant Schemata and Partimento

Melody and bass patterns (Do-Re-Mi, Prinner, Sol-Fa-Mi, Meyer, Fonte) and the partimento bass options.

  • Robert O. Gjerdingen, Music in the Galant Style (New York: Oxford University Press, 2007).
  • Job IJzerman, Harmony, Counterpoint, Partimento (New York: Oxford University Press, 2018).
  • Giorgio Sanguinetti, The Art of Partimento: History, Theory, and Practice (New York: Oxford University Press, 2012).
  • Essays on Music (essaysonmusic.com): articles on the Prinner, the Do-Re-Mi, the Meyer and the Fonte.

Historical Performance

Suggested articulations: slurs, detached notes, tenuto and breath lifts.

  • Carl Philipp Emanuel Bach, Essay on the True Art of Playing Keyboard Instruments, trans. and ed. William J. Mitchell (New York: Norton, 1949). Cited as CPE.
  • Johann Joachim Quantz, On Playing the Flute, trans. Edward R. Reilly (London: Faber and Faber, 1966). Cited as Q.
  • Leopold Mozart, A Treatise on the Fundamental Principles of Violin Playing, trans. Editha Knocker, 2nd ed. (London: Oxford University Press, 1951). Cited as M.

Engraving and Sound

Used on every page.

  • Verovio 6.4.1: music engraving (LGPL-3.0).
  • Nice-Steinway v3.9 sound font (soundfonts4u, 2022, the latest version), built on Anthony Deaton’s Philharmonic piano from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios recordings: piano samples (CC BY-ND 3.0).
  • Versilian Community Sample Library: Italian, Flemish and French harpsichord samples (CC0).
