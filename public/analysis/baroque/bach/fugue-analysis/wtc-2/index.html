Bach · Well-Tempered Clavier II · Fugue Analysis

The 24 Fugues of the Well-Tempered Clavier, Book II

One page per fugue: the subject judged as fugue material, a form map, schemas and cadences, Scott Roberts’s six steps, and an open score with entry colors, an HIP articulation layer and Busoni’s marks. Playback uses recorded samples (Steinway Grand, or an Italian harpsichord at A = 415).

  • Automatic first reading: all 24. A program found the entries, answers, countersubjects, cadences and sequences; it follows only the first subject of the double and triple fugues and can miss entries Bach alters, and the pages say so. Busoni’s tempos and marks were read from his edition by hand, on every entry of the subject and countersubject.
  • Busoni’s swaps: he moved the E♭ major and G major fugues (BWV 876, 884) into his edition of Book I; their pages take his marks from there.
