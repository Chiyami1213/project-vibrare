# Well-Tempered Clavier II: Fugue Analysis

Open `index.html` in a browser. It links all 24 fugue pages (BWV 870–893).

Keep every file in this folder together: the pages load their sound samples from `sounds.js`.

- All 24 pages are an automatic first reading: entries, answers, countersubjects, cadences and sequences were found by a program, which follows only the first subject of the double and triple fugues. Busoni's tempos and marks were read from his edition by hand, entry by entry: each page has a table of what he prints on every entry of the subject, and his countersubject marks are on the score. Each page says what is automatic.
- Busoni moved BWV 876 and 884 into his edition of Book I; their pages take his marks from there.
- Notes: David Huron's Humdrum encoding of the Bach-Gesellschaft edition (BG 14). Scores engraved with Verovio.
- Playback: recorded samples, Steinway Grand by default or an Italian harpsichord at A = 415.
