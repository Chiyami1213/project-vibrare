Bach · Well-Tempered Clavier I · Fugue Analysis

The 24 Fugues of the Well-Tempered Clavier, Book I

One page per fugue: the subject judged as fugue material, a form map, schemas and cadences, Scott Roberts’s six steps, and an open score with entry colors, an HIP articulation layer and Busoni’s marks. Playback uses recorded samples (Steinway Grand, or an Italian harpsichord at A = 415).

  • Checked by hand: C major and C minor (BWV 846–847). Their notes were compared with a second edition and their analysis written by hand.
  • Automatic first reading: the other 22. A program found the entries, answers, countersubjects, cadences and sequences; it can miss entries Bach alters, and the pages say so. Busoni’s tempos and marks were read from his edition by hand, on every entry of the subject and countersubject.
  • Busoni’s swaps: his Book I prints Book II’s E♭ and G major fugues in place of BWV 852 and 860; those two pages use the original fugues and take his marks from where he printed them in Book II.
