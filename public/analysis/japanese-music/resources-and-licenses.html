Source Books

Page numbers in the guides are the printed book pages. Content from Malm, Miki or Kawase that goes beyond Tokita sits in the text with its source chip; longer additions are in grey “beyond the main source” boxes.

Tokita and Hughes (main source)
Alison McQueen Tokita and David W. Hughes, eds., The Ashgate Research Companion to Japanese Music (Aldershot: Ashgate, 2017).
Cited as “Tokita” with the printed page number. The frame of every chapter.
Copyrighted, paraphrased with short quotes
Malm
William P. Malm, Traditional Japanese Music and Musical Instruments, new ed. (Tokyo: Kodansha International, 2000).
Cited as “Malm”. Instrument detail, history and scale figures.
Copyrighted, paraphrased with short quotes
Miki
Minoru Miki, Composing for Japanese Instruments, trans. Marty Regan, ed. Philip Flavin (Rochester, NY: University of Rochester Press, 2008).
Cited as “Miki”. Scales, instrument ranges and playing techniques.
Copyrighted, paraphrased with short quotes
Kawase
Akihiro Kawase, “Regional Classification of Traditional Japanese Folk Songs from Southwest Regions” (Doshisha University), 4 pp.
Cited as “Kawase”. Koizumi tetrachords in regional folk song.
Copyrighted, paraphrased with short quotes

Sounds

Piano (Steinway Grand)
The “Steinway Grand” preset of Nice-Steinway v3.9 by soundfonts4u, built on Anthony Deaton’s set from the University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios piano recordings.
Recorded samples, played back unaltered (a low-pass filter follows the sound font’s own velocity setting).
CC BY-ND 3.0
Koto, Shamisen and Shakuhachi
Musyng Kite sound font, as rendered to per-note MP3 files by the open-source gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts project on GitHub.
Recorded samples, unaltered. Chosen over FluidR3 GM and FatBoy after a listening comparison.
CC BY-SA 3.0

Other Notes

Japanese script (kanji and kana)
The source books print Japanese words in romanization only. The guides add the standard written form after the first mention of each term in a section, from standard usage and checked by web search where uncertain.
This guide series
Text, figures, quizzes and worked examples are compiled for personal study by cross-reading the books above. The share-alike and no-derivatives terms of the sound licenses apply to those samples only.
