Instrument samples
==================

All recordings come from the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain dedication). No attribution is required; it is given here as a courtesy.
https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL

  harpsichord/           Harpsichord, Italian (Sustains, stop 1)
  harpsichord-flemish/   Harpsichord, Flemish (Sustains, low register)
  piano-steinway/        Grand Piano, Steinway B (sustain pedal, medium velocity, close microphones)
  piano-kawai/           Grand Piano, Kawai (sustains, medium velocity)
  organ-renaissance/     Renaissance Organ (8-foot stop, room microphones)

Processing for this site: each recording was trimmed (4.6 to 7 seconds) with a fade at the end, level-matched,
converted to mono MP3 (56 to 64 kbps), and its sounding pitch was measured so every note can be played exactly
in tune (manifest.json in each folder lists the files and their measured pitches). In the Kawai, Flemish, Italian
and organ sets the recordings sound one octave above their file labels; the measured pitches account for that.
