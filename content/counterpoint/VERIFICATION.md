# Verification: Counterpoint (Fux) pack

- **Source:** J. J. Fux, *Gradus ad Parnassum* (1725), trans. Alfred Mann, *The Study of Counterpoint* (Norton, 1965) — the PDF you supplied. The study guide was written chapter by chapter from it (table-of-contents order); every rule and guideline carries a Mann page or figure reference (`refs`).
- **Rules vs guidelines:** a rule is a prohibition or requirement Fux states as binding; a guideline is advice, taste or "if possible". Where Fux bends a guideline in his own models, the guide says so.
- **Examples:** 22 scores. Those labelled "Fig. N" are Fux's figures; "After Fig. N" are re-notated demonstrations of the point Fux makes (e.g. his deliberate mistakes); "Model" scores were generated for the guide by the rule engine and checked by it.
- **Engine results:** every example was run through `engine/fux-engine.js` while building this pack (`engineResult`). Errors appear only in the three examples that show mistakes on purpose (After Fig. 6, After Figs. 26–32, After Fig. 81); the models have no errors. Warnings in Fux's own figures reflect places where Fux bends his own guidelines (e.g. direct motion in three and four parts "if there is no other possibility").
- **Notation:** modern treble / octave-treble / bass clefs replace Fux's C clefs; pitches are unchanged.
